newClip

Syntax:

newClip (length);
newClip (length, loop);

Purpose:

Makes a new, empty clip of length ticks (frames of the game). With loop TRUE, the clip goes round and round; without it, or with FALSE, it plays once and holds what it shows at its end.

Fill it with clipSprite, clipFrames and clipEvent, then use it as any animation: with animate, costume, addCharacter or setCursor. Once used, it can't change. 

var wave = newClip (30);
clipSprite (wave, "", 0, 'ego.duc', 12);
clipSprite (wave, "", 10, 'ego.duc', 13);
clipSprite (wave, "", 20, 'ego.duc', 12);
pause (animate (ego, wave));

Return value:

The new clip.
