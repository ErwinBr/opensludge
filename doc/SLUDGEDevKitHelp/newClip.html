newClip

Syntax:

newClip (length);
newClip (length, loop);

Purpose:

Makes a new, empty animation clip of length ticks (screen updates). With loop TRUE, the clip plays over and over; without it, or with FALSE, it plays once and then holds its last frame. A clip of length 0 shows one fixed pose: useful for standing still.

Fill it with clipSprite, clipKey, clipFrames and clipEvent, then use it as you would an animation of anim: with animate, costume, addCharacter, setCursor or pasteImage, or play it on a layer with playClip. Add all its keys and events first: as soon as you use the clip in one of those ways, it is fixed, and adding a key or event to it gives an error. 

var wave = newClip (30);
clipSprite (wave, "", 0, 'ego.duc', 12);
clipSprite (wave, "", 10, 'ego.duc', 13);
clipSprite (wave, "", 20, 'ego.duc', 12);
pause (animate (ego, wave));

Return value:

The new clip.
