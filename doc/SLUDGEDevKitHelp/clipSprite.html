clipSprite

Syntax:

clipSprite (clip, part, tick, spriteBank, sprite);
clipSprite (clip, part, tick, NOSPRITE);
clipSprite (clip, part, tick, PART_DEFAULT);

Purpose:

From tick on, until its next key, the part of the character shows sprite of the sprite bank spriteBank. A negative sprite shows its mirror image, as with anim. A key at the same tick as another replaces it.

With NOSPRITE, the part shows nothing from tick on: an empty frame. With PART_DEFAULT, it shows its own sprite (a character of one part has none, and shows nothing too).

A character of one sprite has one part: the character itself, named ""; see Composite Characters for characters of more. The tick lies from 0 to the length of the clip. Before its first key, a part shows the sprite of its first key. 

var c = newClip (40, TRUE);
clipSprite (c, "", 0, 'ego.duc', 4);
clipSprite (c, "", 36, 'ego.duc', 5);	# blinks for 4 ticks
clipSprite (c, "", 38, NOSPRITE);

Return value:

None.
