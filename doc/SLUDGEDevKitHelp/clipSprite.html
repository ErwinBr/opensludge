clipSprite

Syntax:

clipSprite (clip, part, tick, spriteBank, sprite);
clipSprite (clip, part, tick, NOSPRITE);
clipSprite (clip, part, tick, PART_DEFAULT);

Purpose:

Changes the displayed sprite from a spriteBank. From tick until the next key of that part, the part of the character shows sprite in spriteBank. A negative sprite shows its mirror image, as with anim.

With NOSPRITE, the part shows nothing: an empty frame. With PART_DEFAULT, it shows the sprite you gave the part with addPart (see Composite Characters).

Sprites never ease: the part changes sprite exactly at the key's tick. 

var c = newClip (40, TRUE);
clipSprite (c, "eyes", 0, 'eyes.duc', 4);
clipSprite (c, "eyes", 36, 'eyes.duc', 5);	# eyes half-closed
clipSprite (c, "eyes", 38, NOSPRITE);	# eyes closed means we show nothing

Return value:

None.
