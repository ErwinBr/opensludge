setPartDepth

Syntax:

setPartDepth (rig, name, depth);

Purpose:

Sets the order in which a part of the rig is drawn among the other parts of the same character. Parts with a lower depth are drawn first, behind the others; parts of the same depth are drawn in the order you added them. Every part starts at 0.

This is the part's usual depth. A clip can change it while it plays with PART_DEPTH (see clipKey), for example to bring the far arm forward when the character turns. Depth only orders the parts of one character: where the character stands among other characters and behind the scenery still depends on its position, as always. Call setPartDepth while you build the rig, before you give it to a character with setCharacterRig: after that the rig can't change.

In the Character Creator, this is the Depth of a part in the Inspector. 

setPartDepth (r, "arm", -1);	# draw the arm behind the torso

Return value:

None.
