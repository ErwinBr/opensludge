setPartDepth

Syntax:

setPartDepth (rig, name, depth);

Purpose:

Sets the order in which a part is drawn: parts of a lower depth first, behind the others. Parts of the same depth are drawn in the order they were added. Every part starts at 0. Clips can change it with PART_DEPTH (see clipKey).

Return value:

None.
