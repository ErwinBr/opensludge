mirrorClip

Syntax:

mirrorClip (clip);

Purpose:

Makes a new clip that is the mirror image of clip: the character itself (the part "") flips where clip has it flip or not. A costume can walk west with the clip that walks east. The new clip can change, as a clip of newClip can. 

costume (standSouth, standWest, standNorth, standEast,
	walkSouth, mirrorClip (walkEast), walkNorth, walkEast,
	talkSouth, talkWest, talkNorth, talkEast)

Return value:

The new clip.
