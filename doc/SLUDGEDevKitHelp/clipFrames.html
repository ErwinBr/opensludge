clipFrames

Syntax:

clipFrames (clip, part, animation);

Purpose:

Makes the frames of an animation of anim the sprites of part in clip, from the start of the clip to its end, with the animation's sounds and functions as events. It replaces the part's sprite keys.

The clip shows and fires exactly what the animation would, tick by tick: frames with a sound or function take no time and aren't drawn, a frame of 0 ticks holds to the end of the clip, and the animation goes round as it always does. Give the clip a length of whole passes of the animation for a loop to match it pass after pass. 

var quack = newClip (10, TRUE);	# two passes of 5 ticks
clipFrames (quack, "", anim ('duck.duc', 0, quackSound, 1, 2, wait (3, 2)));

Return value:

None.
