playClip

Syntax:

playClip (character, layer, clip);
playClip (character, layer, clip, LAYER_ADD);

Purpose:

Plays clip on a layer of a character. You can assign layers 1 to 99. A character's costume exists on layer 0. The layering enables you to play clips on top of other clips, overriding them or adding to them.

An override layer (LAYER_OVERRIDE, the default value) sets what its clip keys, over the layers below it. Parts and properties that its clip doesn't key are left to the layers below. With LAYER_ADD, it adds to a current position (PART_X, PART_Y) and angle (PART_ROTATION), and multiplies a size (PART_SCALE, where 100 leaves a part as it is at 100%). Sprites, visibility, depth and flipping can't be added to: even on an additive layer they are set, and the highest layer that keys them wins.

Playing a clip on a layer that already has one replaces it. The layer has its own time, speed (see setClipSpeed) and events.

A clip that doesn't loop will hold its last key until you end the clip with stopClip. This will end a looping clip as well. 

playClip (hero, 1, breathe (), LAYER_ADD);
playClip (hero, 2, blink ());
pause (playClip (hero, 3, wave ()));
stopClip (hero, 3);

Return value:

The length of the clip, in ticks, as animate gives.
