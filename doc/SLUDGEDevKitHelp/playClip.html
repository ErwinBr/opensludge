playClip

Syntax:

playClip (character, layer, clip);
playClip (character, layer, clip, LAYER_ADD);

Purpose:

Plays clip, from its start, on a layer of character of its own, from 1 to 99, over the animation of its costume (layer 0, which animate, walking and talking change) and over the layers below it. Each layer plays on its own: its time, its speed (setClipSpeed) and its events. A layer that plays another clip starts again.

A layer overrides (LAYER_OVERRIDE, if none is given) what its clip keys: the eyes of a blink, whatever the costume does with them. With LAYER_ADD, it adds to places (PART_X, PART_Y) and angles (PART_ROTATION), and multiplies sizes (PART_SCALE, where 100 leaves a part as it is): breathing, over standing, walking or talking. Sprites, visibility, depth and flipping have nothing to add: any layer sets them, and where two layers set a part's sprite, the higher layer's shows.

A clip that doesn't loop holds its end until stopClip. 

playClip (hero, 1, breathe (), LAYER_ADD);
playClip (hero, 2, blink ());
pause (playClip (hero, 3, wave ()));
stopClip (hero, 3);

Return value:

The length of the clip, in ticks, as animate gives.
