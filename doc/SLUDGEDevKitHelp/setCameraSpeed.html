setCameraSpeed

Syntax:

setCameraSpeed (speed, easeIn, easeOut);

Purpose:

Sets how the camera moves when it follows a character (trackCharacter) or glides to a point (panCamera).

speed is the most pixels the camera moves in a frame. A speed of 0 (as when the game starts) sticks the camera to the character it follows, with no lag at all, and makes panCamera jump straight there.

easeIn is how many frames the camera takes to speed up from standing still to its full speed, and easeOut how many frames it takes to slow down again before it stops. Use 0 for either to start or stop at full speed. Longer ease times give a softer, more film-like camera. With an ease-out, a camera following a walking character stays a few pixels behind it, so that it's ready to slow down when the character stops.

None of the numbers can be below 0. The settings stay until you change them, through every call to trackCharacter and panCamera.

Return value:

No return value.

Example:

sub init () {
   # Up to 4 pixels a frame. At 20 frames a second, it takes
   # half a second to speed up, and a second to slow down.
   setCameraSpeed (4, 10, 20);
}
