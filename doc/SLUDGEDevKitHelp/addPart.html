addPart

Syntax:

addPart (rig, name, parent, x, y);
addPart (rig, name, parent, x, y, spriteBank, sprite);

Purpose:

Adds a part called name to rig, attached to the part called parent ("" for the character itself), with its origin (its sprite's hotspot) at x, y from its parent's origin. The part shows sprite of spriteBank until a clip gives it another; a negative sprite shows its mirror image.

Without a sprite, the part is a joint: it shows nothing, but places what is attached to it, as a hand that holds things. 

addPart (r, "head", "torso", 3, -213, 'hero_head.duc', 0);
addPart (r, "hand", "arm", -30, 222);

Return value:

None.
