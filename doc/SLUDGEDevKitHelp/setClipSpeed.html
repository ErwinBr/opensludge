setClipSpeed

Syntax:

setClipSpeed (character, layer, percent);

Purpose:

Plays the animation of character at percent of its normal speed, on the given layer. 100 is normal, 50 half speed, 200 twice as fast, and 0 pauses it. It works for clips, and for animations created with anim. 

setClipSpeed (ego, 0, 50);	# slow motion

Return value:

TRUE, or FALSE if there's no such character or nothing plays on that layer.
