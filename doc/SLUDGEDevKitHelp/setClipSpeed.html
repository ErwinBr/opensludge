setClipSpeed

Syntax:

setClipSpeed (character, layer, percent);

Purpose:

Plays the animation of character at percent of its normal speed: 100 is normal, 50 half speed, 200 twice as fast, and 0 pauses it. It works for clips and for animations of anim, and stays until changed again.

Characters have one layer for now: 0, the animation of their costume, or of animate. 

setClipSpeed (ego, 0, 50);	# slow motion

Return value:

TRUE if there is such a character, FALSE if not.
