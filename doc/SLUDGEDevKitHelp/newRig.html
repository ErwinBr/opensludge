newRig

Syntax:

newRig ();

Purpose:

Makes a new rig: the parts of a character, and what is attached to what. It has one part to begin with, the character itself, called "". Add parts with addPart, then give it to a character with setCharacterRig. Once a character has it, it can't change.

Return value:

The new rig.
