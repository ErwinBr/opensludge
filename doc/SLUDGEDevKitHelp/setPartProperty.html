setPartProperty

Syntax:

setPartProperty (character, part, property, value);

Purpose:

Sets a property of a part of character to value, over every layer, until clearPartProperty: a head that looks at the mouse, an arm that holds something up. The properties and their units are those of clipKey: PART_X, PART_Y, PART_ROTATION, PART_SCALE (in percent), PART_DEPTH, PART_VISIBLE and PART_FLIP. 

setPartProperty (hero, "head", PART_ROTATION, -15);

Return value:

TRUE if there is such a character, FALSE if not.
