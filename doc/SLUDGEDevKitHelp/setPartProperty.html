setPartProperty

Syntax:

setPartProperty (character, part, property, value);

Purpose:

Sets a property of a part of character directly from your script, for example a head that turns to follow the mouse, or an arm that holds something up. The value wins over every clip and layer, and stays until you call clearPartProperty.

The properties and their units are those of clipKey: PART_X, PART_Y, PART_ROTATION, PART_SCALE (in percent), PART_DEPTH, PART_VISIBLE and PART_FLIP. Sprites can't be set this way; play a clip with clipSprite on a layer instead (see playClip). 

setPartProperty (hero, "head", PART_ROTATION, -15);	# tilt the head
...
clearPartProperty (hero, "head", PART_ROTATION);	# the clips move the head again

Return value:

TRUE if there is such a character, FALSE if not.
