clipKey

Syntax:

clipKey (clip, part, property, tick, value);
clipKey (clip, part, property, tick, value, easing);

Purpose:

From tick on, the property of part moves towards value, which it reaches at the next key:

  • PART_X, PART_Y: where the part's origin is, in pixels from its parent's
  • PART_ROTATION: how far it is turned, in degrees, clockwise
  • PART_SCALE: its size, in percent, and that of all attached to it
  • PART_DEPTH: the order of drawing (see setPartDepth)
  • PART_VISIBLE: TRUE or FALSE; FALSE hides the part and all attached to it
  • PART_FLIP: TRUE mirrors the part and all attached to it

The easing says how it moves towards the next key: EASE_LINEAR (straight there, if none is given), EASE_INOUT (easing in and out) or EASE_STEP (it keeps its value until the next key). Depth, visibility and flipping always step. Before its first key a part has the value of its first key; after its last key, the value of its last. A key at the length of a clip closes a loop: the last stretch moves towards it. 

var bob = newClip (48, TRUE);
clipKey (bob, "torso", PART_Y, 0, -250);
clipKey (bob, "torso", PART_Y, 24, -262, EASE_INOUT);
clipKey (bob, "torso", PART_Y, 48, -250, EASE_INOUT);

Return value:

None.
