setCharacterRig

Syntax:

setCharacterRig (character, rig);
setCharacterRig (character, 0);

Purpose:

Makes character a Composite Character: from now on it draws the parts of its rig. With 0 instead of a rig, character goes back to showing a single sprite, as a normal character. After setting this, you can't change the rig itself anymore, only update its properties through Animation Clips, or by setting them directly with setPartProperty.

The clips of the costume, and those you give to animate, then key the parts by name. An animation of anim shows on the character as a whole (the root).

Return value:

TRUE if there is such a character, FALSE if not.
